Some authors use formulas of Peano Arithmetic as a proxy for algorithms that implement Prisoner's Dilemma's strategies (i.e., in a Turing-complete model of computation such as a general purpose programming language). THis works via known Church-Turing Thesis correspondences. In the case where Peano Arithmetic is used, Carnap's Diagonalisation Lemma is the arithmetic equivalent of Kleene's Recursion Theorem.
